02 · ABOUT

Turning thoughtful ideas into designs made to work in the real world.

I’m a Packaging Designer who blends creative thinking, visual design, and production awareness to shape packaging experiences with clarity and intent.

My work is grounded in careful observation, thoughtful composition, and a practical understanding of how design performs in print, production, and real-world retail environments. I focus on packaging that feels distinctive, useful, and considered from the first concept through final production.

With a background in Printing, Publishing & Packaging, I approach each project with attention to detail, print awareness, prepress knowledge, and a strong sense of production realities. The result is design that is not only visually compelling, but also technically informed and commercially relevant.
